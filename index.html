Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Image Compressor & Size Reducer
("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App.
The App is developed and maintained by Ruhaan(ENIGMA DEVS).

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Photos and Files You Select

The App processes images and PDF files entirely on your device.
We do NOT upload, transmit, or store your photos, PDFs, or any
content you process. Your files never leave your phone.

1.2 Locally Stored Preferences

The App stores a small amount of data on your device only:
- Your dark mode / light mode preference
- Your Privacy Mode toggle state
- Your Pro purchase status (to hide ads after purchase)
- Your selected country (for localized presets and pricing)

This data is stored in the App's private storage on your device
and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

1.3 Information Collected by Third-Party Services

GOOGLE ADMOB (ADVERTISING)

If you are using the free version of the App, Google AdMob may
collect:
- Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on Android)
- Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
- IP address and approximate location (city-level)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

AdMob uses this data to serve ads and measure their performance.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your
device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
AdMob data usage: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

GOOGLE PLAY BILLING (IN-APP PURCHASE)

If you purchase the Pro upgrade, Google Play processes the payment.
We never see or store your payment card details, billing address,
or Google account password. Google Play returns only a purchase
token and confirmation that the purchase succeeded.

Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/
